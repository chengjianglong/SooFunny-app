# SooFunny Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 16, 2026_

SooFunny is a learning and coloring app for children, made by Chengjiang Long
(https://www.chengjianglong.com/). © 2026 Soo X Intelligence LLC. All rights reserved.

## The short version

**SooFunny collects no personal information. Everything happens on your device.**
There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers that receive your data.

## What stays on your device

- **Pictures.** Photos you choose or take are turned into coloring pages on the device.
  They are never uploaded. A colored picture leaves the device only if you choose to share or
  save it with the system Share button.
- **Voice.** When you tap Talk, the microphone is used to hear your question. Speech is turned
  into text with Apple's on-device speech recognition and is never recorded or saved. For
  Chinese, iOS may download Apple's Mandarin speech model once; only the model is downloaded, and
  nothing you say is sent anywhere.
- **The talking buddy.** Answers come from an AI model (Qwen 2.5) that runs entirely on
  the device. Questions and answers are not sent anywhere and are forgotten when you leave the
  conversation.
- **Settings and progress.** Your voice settings and math progress are stored only on the
  device and are deleted when you delete the app. Math progress can be erased in
  Math → Math Settings.

## Checking for updates

When the app opens, it may ask Apple's App Store for the latest version number of SooFunny, at
most every few hours. Only the app's App Store ID and the country or region of the store are
sent; nothing about you, your child or the device. If a newer version exists, the app offers it,
and opening the App Store is behind the grown-up check.

## Purchases

SooFunny is free. Grown-ups can choose to leave an optional, one-time tip, sold by Apple through
the App Store. Payment and billing are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment
details. Tips unlock nothing, and the app keeps no record of them. Former subscribers to
SooFunny Premium can see in the app whether their old subscription is still set to renew. See
Apple's privacy policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

## Children

SooFunny is designed for children and complies with the App Store's rules for kids' apps. It
does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Links that leave the app
are behind a grown-up check, and tips go through the App Store's own purchase sheet, which asks
for Face ID or the account password.

## Permissions

SooFunny asks for permission to use the camera, the microphone, speech recognition and to save
to your photo library, and only when you use those features. You can change these at any time in
the device's Settings → Privacy & Security.

## On Android

The Android app, from Google Play, follows the same rules: no accounts, no ads, no analytics,
no tracking, and nothing you make or say leaves the device. The differences are:

- **The talking buddy's model is downloaded once.** The buddy's AI model (Qwen 2.5, about
  1.1 GB) is too big to come with the app, so a grown-up downloads it from Hugging Face
  (huggingface.co) on the buddy screen. Apart from tips and update checks (below), this is the
  only time the Android app uses the internet.
  Only the model is downloaded: nothing about you or your child is sent, and Hugging Face sees
  only an ordinary download request from the device. The model can be deleted in Settings.
- **Voice.** When you tap Talk, speech is turned into text with the phone's own on-device speech
  recognition (Android 12 or later). SooFunny never uses an online speech service, so if the
  phone has no on-device recognizer for the language, the child types the question instead.
  Nothing said is recorded or saved.
- **Reading pages and photos** uses Google's ML Kit on the device (text recognition, the
  document scanner and subject detection). SooFunny turns off ML Kit's usage logging, so
  pictures and words stay on the device. Google Play services may download ML Kit's models.
- **Books you scan** and their audiobooks are kept in the app's own storage and are deleted when
  you delete the app. An audiobook leaves the device only if a grown-up shares it.
- **Purchases.** As on iOS, grown-ups can leave the same optional, one-time tips, sold by Google
  through Google Play. Payment and billing are handled entirely by Google; we never see your
  payment details. Tips unlock nothing, and the app keeps no record of them. Google Play's own
  purchase sheet confirms each payment. See Google's privacy policy at
  https://policies.google.com/privacy.
- **Checking for updates.** When the app opens, at most every few hours, it asks Google Play,
  through the Play Store app on the phone, whether a newer version of SooFunny is out. Nothing
  about you, your child or the device is sent by SooFunny. If there is one, the app offers it,
  and updating is behind the grown-up check.
- **Permissions.** Android asks for the microphone when you first tap Talk. Photos are picked with
  the system photo picker and the camera app, so SooFunny sees only the pictures you choose.
  Saved pictures go to Pictures/SooFunny.
