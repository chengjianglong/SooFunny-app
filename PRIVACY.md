# SooFunny Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

SooFunny is a learning and coloring app for children, made by Chengjiang Long
(https://www.chengjianglong.com/). © 2026 Soo X Intelligence Inc. All rights reserved.

## The short version

**SooFunny collects no personal information. Everything happens on your device.**
There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers that receive your data.

## What stays on your device

- **Pictures.** Photos you choose or take are turned into coloring pages on the device.
  They are never uploaded. A colored picture leaves the device only if you choose to share or
  save it with the system Share button.
- **Voice.** When you tap Talk, the microphone is used to hear your question. Speech is turned
  into text with Apple's on-device speech recognition and is never recorded or saved.
- **The talking buddy.** Answers come from an AI model (Google's Gemma) that runs entirely on
  the device. Questions and answers are not sent anywhere and are forgotten when you leave the
  conversation.
- **Settings and progress.** Your voice settings and math progress are stored only on the
  device and are deleted when you delete the app. Math progress can be erased in
  Math → Math Settings.

## Purchases

SooFunny Premium is an auto-renewing subscription sold by Apple through the App Store. Payment
and billing are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. Apple tells the
app only whether the subscription is active. See Apple's privacy policy at
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

## Children

SooFunny is designed for children and complies with the App Store's rules for kids' apps. It
does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases and links that
leave the app are behind a grown-up check.

## Permissions

SooFunny asks for permission to use the camera, the microphone, speech recognition and to save
to your photo library, and only when you use those features. You can change these at any time in
the device's Settings → Privacy & Security.
